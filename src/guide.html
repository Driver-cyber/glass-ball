How it works
Type it. Tap it.
That’s the manual.

Type on the empty line — now it’s a ball on your day. Tap the checkbox — caught, struck through, stickered. Tap the ball itself and it offers everything else it can do: ▶ run a timer · → move to another day · ✎ edit.
You have now read the entire manual.

The timer
25:00
🎭

“Somewhere a version of you already finished this. Go meet her.”

— the app, every time you press Start. Always skippable.

Tap ▶ on any ball and its minutes load the moon — half-lit means halfway. Close the window; it keeps running on a little bar at the bottom of every room. It opens with a joke, because chores don’t deserve solemnity — and it ends with one honest question: did you finish? “Still needs work” buys more minutes, or hands the rest to another day on purpose. Nothing dropped. Nothing hanging over you.
