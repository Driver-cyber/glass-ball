The timer
25:00

Tap a ball, tap ▶ Start it, and the moon keeps its time — half-lit means halfway. Close the window and it keeps running; a little bar rides along the bottom until you tap it again.

It opens with a joke (always skippable) and ends with the only honest question: did you finish? Say “still needs work” and it offers more minutes — or rolls the rest onto another day, on purpose.

Didn’t finish and you’re done for today? Every ball offers → Move it forward — hand it to another day. It stays here, settled, and lands there for real. Nothing dropped, nothing hanging.
